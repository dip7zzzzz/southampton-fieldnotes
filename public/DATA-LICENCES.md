Its graph is encoded as documented in src/lib/codec.ts in the accompanying
repository. Retain attribution and comply with applicable database share-alike
requirements when redistributing it.

Resident population context: Office for National Statistics, via Nomis.
Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence
v3.0, with applicable Crown copyright. Holiday dates: GOV.UK, OGL v3.0.
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/

University term dates are extracted calendar facts with source links in the
dataset. Full university page text and private raw caches are not published.

Geographic residential context uses ONS/Nomis PESTOA2021 (2024) and matching
MSOA2021 BGC V3 boundaries. Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under
the Open Government Licence v.3.0. Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database
right 2024. The cell-centre join supplies aggregate MSOA context, not measured
125-metre demographics or visitor composition.

University of Southampton building geometry and mapped entrance/exit data use the
publisher's [Open Government Licence](http://reference.data.gov.uk/id/open-government-licence).
Sources: [Places](https://data.southampton.ac.uk/dataset/places.html) and
[Building Entrances and Exits](https://data.southampton.ac.uk/dataset/building-portals.html).
Portals are non-authoritative; current access, use and freshness are unverified.
These sources add geometry/provenance, not attendance or footfall observations.

These records are not measured footfall or visitor demographics. The uniform
resident prior does not establish local women-aged-18–38 visitor composition.

Application source is publicly viewable for this deployment. No open-source
licence for the original application code is granted by publishing it.
